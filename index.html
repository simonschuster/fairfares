Lodging: Reimbursed as actuals up to $333/night pre-tax. Receipts required.
Meals: Actual costs only, up to $92/day. Receipts are not required for any individual meal under $75. If the traveler claims the full $92 daily M&IE cap without receipts for 3 days, receipts are required from day 4 onward to show the cap is not being treated as a per diem.
Source: UCOP G-28 Travel Policy.

DoD per diem rates — this destination

ComponentAmount (USD)How it works
Maximum lodging—Nightly hotel cap. Claimed as actuals up to this limit. May exceed up to 300% with exceptional approver approval and receipts.
Daily M&IE—Daily meals & incidentals allowance. No receipts required when claiming per diem rate.
Maximum per diem—Lodging + M&IE combined daily maximum.
Actuals vs per diem For international travel, M&IE and lodging are separate elections. Travelers may use per diem for either, with no receipts required, or claim actual expenses. When claiming actuals, receipts are required for individual M&IE expenses of $75 or more and for all lodging expenses. Foreign lodging may exceed the per diem rate in special circumstances, up to 300% of the applicable rate, with your school's exceptional approver's approval and receipts.

Source: US Department of Defense — travel.dod.mil.
